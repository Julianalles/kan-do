### en

- **A new app icon.** A stack of cards with “Kan do” and a check on forest green: you spot Kan-do in the Dock at a glance.
- **Kan-do on your home screen.** In the browser you install Kan-do as an app of its own, and on your phone you add it to the home screen with the new icon.
